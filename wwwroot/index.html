SLSU BSME Alumni Information System

The SLSU BSME Alumni Information System updates, monitors, and tracks the career progression of Mechanical Engineering graduates from Southern Luzon State University. It maintains strong alumni connections and provides valuable insights into professional achievements, employment status, and mechanical engineer licensure examination performance.


Data is gathered through Google Forms filled out by alumni and is automatically reflected in this system in real time.
